# Privacy Policy

Element for HarmonyOS embeds the open-source element-web client. The app does not
collect, store, or process any personal data, and does not send any data to the
developer's servers.

Your messages, account information, and files are transmitted only to the Matrix
homeserver you choose to connect to (default: matrix.org) and are governed by that
homeserver's own terms. End-to-end encrypted content cannot be read by the app or
its developer.

Data remains on your device in the app's sandbox and is deleted when you uninstall
the app. Notifications appear only while the app is running.

This app is provided "AS IS" WITHOUT WARRANTY OF ANY KIND, express or implied.
